## B-5. Label-delay feasibility table  (blueprint §9.6 — referenced by PLAN.md T4.5)

Columns: `Evaluation cohort | Delay | Eligible calibration loans | Defaults |
Non-defaults | Feasible`. Minimum total sample size alone is insufficient —
both default and non-default minimums must hold (values in
`configs/calibration.yaml`: intercept/Platt 200/200, isotonic 500/500,
PROPOSED). Infeasible delays are excluded from confirmatory analysis, never
rescued by weakening thresholds after seeing results.

## B-10. Final QA checklist  (blueprint §22 — referenced by PLAN.md T13.6)

**Research design (5):** gap supported by completed literature review · RQs and
hypotheses defined · protocol frozen before confirmatory test evaluation ·
deviations documented · primary and exploratory analyses distinguished.
**Data integrity (8):** provenance and licence documented · checksum recorded ·
target verified · 36-month maturity rule · quarterly cohorts validated ·
feature availability audit · no post-origination leakage · preprocessing
fitted on training data only.
**Experimental validity (8):** temporal evaluation throughout · configurations
recorded · validation used for selection · test cohorts untouched during
tuning · calibrators fitted on eligible historical labels · label-delay
assumptions documented · explanation axes analysed separately · decision
counterfactual limitations acknowledged.
**Statistical quality (8):** CIs reported · paired comparisons where
appropriate · bootstrap design documented · configuration dependence
addressed · multiple comparisons handled · practical effect sizes considered ·
sensitivity analyses completed · negative/inconclusive results reported
honestly.
**Publication & reproducibility (9):** references verified · figures from
reproducible scripts · tables agree with results · limitations stated ·
supervisor feedback incorporated · manuscript professionally edited · repo
reproduction instructions · credentials/restricted data excluded · citation
and software licences included.

## B-11. Regulatory context (motivation only — blueprint §1.6 note + PLAN.md I-2)

- **17 April 2026**: Fed **SR 26-2**, OCC **Bulletin 2026-13**, FDIC
  **FIL-15-2026** — revised interagency model-risk management guidance that
  *supersedes* SR 11-7 (2011) and SR 21-8. Primary source:
  `federalreserve.gov/supervisionreg/srletters/SR2602.htm`. Note the letter's
  applicability framing (most relevant to banking organisations with >$30B
  assets). SR 11-7 = historical predecessor only. PI verifies text (T1.8).
- **EU AI Act**: creditworthiness assessment of natural persons listed as
  high-risk in Annex III, subject to scope/exceptions — read the consolidated
  text on EUR-Lex, not summaries. Also consider ECOA/Regulation B adverse
  action (primary text). These motivate the paper; they are **not** technical
  prior art.
