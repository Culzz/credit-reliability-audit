## What it does (from abstract)
- Builds three *adaptive SHAP* framework variants for evolving credit data:
  - (A) per-slice explanation reweighting adjusting for feature distribution
    shift;
  - (B) drift-aware SHAP rebaselining with sliding-window background samples;
  - (C) online surrogate calibration via incremental Ridge regression.
- Benchmarks each against static SHAP with XGBoost on a multi-year credit
  dataset (dataset identity UNKNOWN from abstract).
- Stable = improved temporal explanation stability (cosine, Kendall tau) and
  fairness (demographic parity, "recalibration"); claims no accuracy loss.
- Robustness probes: counterfactual perturbation, background sensitivity,
  proxy-variable detection.

calibration/explanation/decision reliability track AUC?). It motivates but
does not occupy our claim: no joint audit, no decision-flip outcomes, no
label-delay recalibration of probabilities. Risk of overlap: none identified
